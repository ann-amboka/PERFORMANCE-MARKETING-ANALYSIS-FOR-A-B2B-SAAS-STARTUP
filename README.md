# Business context

Tallybridge captures, codes, approves, and pays supplier invoices. It is built for Canadian taxes (GST/HST, PST, QST), Canadian payment rails (EFT and Interac e-Transfer), English and French, and Canadian data residency.

## Company snapshot
- **Stage:** Seed (CAD 2.1M raised), launching publicly in Q1 2027
- **Customers today:** 6 free design partners
- **Year 1 goal:** 120 paying customers and CAD 520K ARR
- **Pricing (CAD per month):** Starter 149, Growth 399, Scale 899, Enterprise custom

## Challenges at launch
- No Canadian brand awareness and few referenceable customers
- A trust-heavy sales cycle averaging about 52 days
- No accounting-firm referral channel yet
- A small team and a limited marketing budget

---

1. CLIENT ACQUISITION STRATEGY
## Ideal customer profile
- 20 to 200 employees
- 150 to 2,000 supplier invoices per month
- CAD 5M to 60M annual revenue
- Using QuickBooks Online or Sage 50, often with manual email and spreadsheet processes
- Priority industries: construction and trades, wholesale and distribution, professional services and engineering, multi-location franchises and hospitality

## Five steps
1. **Define the ICP** using company size, invoice volume, revenue, and current systems.
2. **Identify 200 prospects**, 50 per priority industry, with decision-makers (CFOs, controllers, finance managers, owners, COOs).
3. **Run personalized email outreach**, tailored to each industry's invoice challenges.
4. **Run LinkedIn outreach** through existing and secondary connections, with optional short founder-led videos to build trust.
5. **Run paid advertising**, starting with a $1,000 Meta Ads test.

## Four acquisition channels
| Channel | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Outreach** | Audience-led (connections, referrals) and method-led (cold email, LinkedIn DMs) |
| **LinkedIn content** | Posts on manual invoice processing, delayed approvals, tax-coding errors, and reconciliation |
| **Paid advertising** | Days 1-30 Meta awareness, days 31-60 retargeting, days 61-90 Google Search |
| **Community engagement** | Finance and business communities to build credibility and find referral partners |

Full detail: [Client acquisition strategy](https://github.com/ann-amboka/PERFORMANCE-MARKETING-ANALYSIS-FOR-A-B2B-SAAS-STARTUP/blob/main/Tallybridge_Client_Acquisition_Strategy.docx)

2. META ADS CAMPAIGN SETUP
- **Objective:** Traffic, sending prospects to the landing page
- **Ad sets:** 5, built from the ICP, priority industries, and target regions
- **Geography:** Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia, and Quebec (Quebec included for the French-language offering)
- **Placements:** Facebook, Instagram, and Threads
- **Audiences:** the four priority industries, plus accounting firms as a referral-partner audience
- **Budget:** $1,000 over 30 days, framed as a learning test to guide later spend
- **Creatives:** 8 concepts (static, video, and carousel) in 1080x1080 and 1080x1920

## Phased plan
| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| Days 1-30 | Traffic campaigns to test audience interest and build retargeting pools |
| Days 31-60 | Retargeting and prospecting using proof from the 6 design partners |
| Days 61-90 | Google Search plus value-based retargeting to convert high-intent buyers |

## Funnel logic
- **Top:** awareness and traffic to the ICP
- **Middle:** trust-building with testimonials and results from the 6 free design partners, which lowers friction
- **Bottom:** value-based offers (free invoice cost audit, waived onboarding, guided 14-day trial)

# Measurement framework

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| CPM | Spend / Impressions x 1,000 |
| CTR | Clicks / Impressions |
| CPC | Spend / Clicks |
| CPL | Spend / Leads |
| CPA | Spend / Customers |
| ROAS | Revenue / Spend |
| EPA | Revenue / Customers |
| EPL | Revenue / Leads |
| EPC | Revenue / Clicks |

**Decision targets (assumptions to tune):** CTR of at least 1.0%, CPL of at most CAD 70, and CPA at or below the budget divided by the customers needed.

---

3. PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

## Overall results
- **What happened:** $16,000 across Meta (traffic, retargeting, prospecting) and Google Search produced 217 leads at about $74 each and 20 paying customers. Customers grew from 0 to 3 to 17 as the budget and offers matured.
- **Good or bad:** Mixed. CTR met its target in every period, but CPL ($74) is slightly above the $70 target and CPA ($800) is far above the roughly $216 needed to reach 80 customers.
- **Recommendation:** Scale the best performers (the accounting firms ad set, value-offer retargeting, and Google Search) and pause the weakest. Use proof from the 6 design partners in the middle of the funnel, and value-based offers at the bottom: a free invoice cost audit, waived onboarding, and a guided 14-day trial.

# The gap to 80 customers

## The gap
- Paid media produced 20 customers in the sample.
- If all 6 design partners convert to paid, the total is 26.
- That leaves **54 customers** short of the 80 target.

## Required CPA
- A $16,000 budget needs a blended CPA of about **$216** to reach 80.
- The sample CPA is **$800**, roughly 3.7 times too high.

## What each lead-to-customer rate demands
74 customers are needed after the design partners convert.

| Lead-to-customer rate | Leads needed | Max CPL allowed | Budget needed at current CPL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 740 | $22 | $54,562 |
| 15% | 493 | $32 | $36,375 |
| 20% | 370 | $43 | $27,281 |
| 25% | 296 | $54 | $21,825 |
| 35% | 211 | $76 | $15,589 |

## What closes the gap
- Paid media alone is unlikely to reach 80 at the observed acquisition cost.
- Partner referrals (accounting firms), direct outreach, and early conversion of the free design partners are needed alongside ads.
- With a 52-day average sales cycle, leads from late in the window may not close by day 90. A guided 14-day trial can speed this up, backed by fast follow-up.

# Limitations and next steps

## Limitations
- Tallybridge and all results are fictional and simulated.
- Meta cannot target by revenue or invoice volume, so ICP fit is approximated with job titles and interests.
- Attribution depends on correct Meta Pixel events and UTM tagging, which should be in place before launch.
